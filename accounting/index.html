Please review the following requirements before entering the MIRROR research area.

Facility Safety

  • Visitors must remain with their authorized escort while inside restricted areas.
  • Photography and video recording are prohibited inside designated research areas.
  • Do not connect personal devices to facility equipment or networks.
  • Follow posted emergency evacuation procedures.
  • Report unattended devices, suspicious QR labels, or unusual activity to Security.
No authentication is required. Public visitor and safety information should never require your corporate password, MFA code, payment information, or installation of software.
