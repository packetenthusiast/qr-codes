MIRROR Security Awareness

This was a phishing simulation

You attempted to enter credentials into a website reached through an unverified QR code.

No credentials were collected.

The values entered into the previous form were discarded locally by your browser. They were not transmitted, logged, or stored.

What should you have noticed?

A familiar sign-in page does not prove that the website belongs to Microsoft or your organization.

Before entering credentials after scanning a QR code, check the destination and ask whether authentication should actually be required for the resource you were trying to access.

QR codes can conceal phishing links just as easily as links in email.
